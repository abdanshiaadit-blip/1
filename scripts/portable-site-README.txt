HUMAN — the website, offline
============================

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Double-click index.html.

That's it. It opens in your default browser. No install, no terminal,
no internet connection needed. You can move this file anywhere —
Desktop, a USB stick, WhatsApp, email — and it will still work.

Works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox.


HOW TO LOOK AT IT
-----------------
Scroll. Slowly. The whole site is one continuous story and almost
everything on it responds to scroll position rather than to clicks.

The app you see inside the phone is not a picture. It is the real
HUMAN prototype running live, and scrolling drives it — changing tabs,
opening sheets, starting the booking flow. If you stop scrolling in
the app section, you can click around inside the phone yourself.

Two sections are yours to steer rather than to watch:

  Nine systems    Click any body system on the left.
  Explore         Opens the prototype in a new tab (needs internet).

Narrow the window to phone width to see the mobile version, which is
laid out differently rather than just squeezed.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
The site uses recent CSS. On a browser more than about two years old,
some sections will render flat rather than animated. Everything stays
readable.


NOTES
-----
HUMAN is a working prototype. All member data shown is fictional,
prices are planning assumptions, and the Health Intelligence score is
a HUMAN construct rather than a validated clinical measure.

HUMAN supports your health decisions. It does not diagnose conditions
and does not replace your doctor.
