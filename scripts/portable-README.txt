HUMAN — offline demo
====================

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Double-click index.html.

That's it. It opens in your default browser. No install, no terminal,
no internet connection needed. You can move this folder anywhere —
Desktop, a USB stick, a shared drive — and it will still work.

Works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox.


WHAT YOU'LL SEE
---------------
On a laptop, the app appears inside an iPhone frame, with a control to
the left of the phone that switches between two members:

  Aadit Rao   34, Bengaluru — metabolic priority, mid-experiment
  Meera Iyer  31, Mumbai — iron and cycle priority, women's health

Switching members swaps the entire product state, not just a name.

To see it full-screen the way it would appear on a real phone, narrow
your browser window to roughly 450px wide or less.


WORTH LOOKING AT
----------------
Home            The hero card: one score, one stage, one priority.
Action          "Not now" -> "Why" shows what the Decision Engine is
                deliberately holding back, and its reasoning.
Health          Swipe the body-systems card sideways. Tap any biomarker
                for its trend, meaning and Indian context.
Meera           Home -> cycle card, for Women's Longitudinal Health.
Book a test     Home -> "Book a blood test" -> through to Confirm, where
                the loop closes: results land in a changed plan.


NOTES
-----
All member data is fictional. Prices are planning assumptions and are
labelled as such in the app. Payment, lab integration and clinician
workflows are simulated.

HUMAN does not diagnose and does not replace a clinician.
